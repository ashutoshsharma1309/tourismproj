## 1 · WHAT THIS IS, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sikkim Darshan (formerly "SikkimOS", originally scaffolded as "yatra-ai") is a
digital cultural-heritage platform for the Indian state of Sikkim. It holds a
sourced archive of 15 monasteries, 38 heritage and natural places, 70 long-form
cultural stories, a 26-event historical timeline, a 77-object digital heritage
archive with community contribution, 60 narrated audio guides in 4 languages,
an interactive heritage map, a rule-based trip planner, a directory of 20
registered stays, and a public preservation dashboard that reports the
archive's own coverage and gaps. It was built for SIH (Smart India Hackathon).

It is NOT a booking platform, NOT a SaaS product, and NOT an AI content
generator. An earlier build tried to be the first two and faked the data to do
it; that data was deleted rather than relabelled.

## 2 · THE GOVERNING RULE (read this before proposing any feature)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

  > Every factual claim rendered in the product points at an entry in
  > src/data/sources.ts, or it does not ship.

Corollaries that shape almost every design decision in the codebase:

  * Missing facts are PUBLISHED as missing. The UI says "Data not available"
    or "Not yet captured" instead of estimating. /preservation exists to make
    the gaps visible on purpose.
  * NO LANGUAGE MODEL COMPOSES PROSE ANYWHERE IN THE PIPELINE. Every published
    clause is assembled from a verified field on a record, and a clause is
    omitted entirely when its field is absent. This is what makes it
    structurally impossible for a guide to invent a founder, a date or a
    legend the sources do not contain.
  * A missing pin beats a guessed pin. Monasteries with disputed coordinates
    are excluded from the map (`mappableMonasteries` is the map's only input).
  * An empty gallery beats a wrong photograph. See §7 (gallery rejections).
  * Two encyclopedia articles agreeing with each other is ONE tertiary source
    agreeing with itself — it does not count as corroboration. Encyclopedia-
    sourced confidence is capped at "medium" and this is enforced mechanically
    by scripts/qa/heritage-integrity.mjs.
  * Where sources disagree (15 vs 16 May 1975; 8th vs 9th century for Guru
    Rinpoche), the disagreement is written into the record rather than
    resolved by picking a side.

WHAT THIS RULE ALREADY COST THE PROJECT — deleted, not relabelled:
  invented hotel tariffs · star ratings · guest reviews · room inventories ·
  220 synthetic bookings · a fabricated TSD remittance ledger · invented
  festival dates, visiting hours, phone numbers and email addresses ·
  European panoramas reused as Sikkim prayer halls · a fabricated arrivals
  figure (21,47,892) and a fabricated TSD total (₹12.5 Cr).

If you are asked to "add reviews" or "show prices", the answer is a licensed
Places/booking integration, not more mock data.

## 6 · THE FOUR RULES-ENGINES (no ML anywhere in these)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

TSD FEE — src/lib/booking.ts
  Models the Tourism Sustainability Development Fund as the Government of
  Sikkim actually defines it in the Sikkim Registration of Tourist Trade
  Rules, 2025:
    • Flat ₹50 per person, one-time, valid one month
    • Collected by hotels at check-in, deposited to the TSD Fund
    • Children under 5 and government-work visitors exempt
    • Re-entry within the same month is charged again
  The previous implementation charged 2–4% of room tariff. That was invented
  AND it misstated live public policy; it is gone. GST is applied at 12% and
  labelled an estimate, not a quoted rate. computeTariff(), nightsBetween()
  and makeBookingId() ("NEY-HTL-####", deterministic) remain.

TRIP PLANNER — src/lib/generate-itinerary.ts
  Rule-based over real geography. Four anchors — Gangtok, Pelling, Lachung,
  Ravangla — each with interest-tagged day templates (Monasteries, Culture,
  Lakes, Food, Adventure, Trekking, Nature). routePlan() chooses the anchor
  sequence and night allocation from trip length and interests, keeping drive
  times sane; arrival days always sequence first. Duration clamped 3–14 days.
  Cost output deliberately contains ONE line: the statutory TSD fee.
  Accommodation, transport and activity totals are NOT estimated because no
  rates feed is licensed. The return shape (GeneratedItinerary) is the
  contract a model would fill in a later phase.

COMMUNITY SUBMISSIONS — src/lib/archive-submissions.ts
  The one rule this module exists to enforce: a submission NEVER becomes
  published archive content on its own. Every record is created with status
  "pending-review" and THERE IS NO CODE PATH IN THE APPLICATION THAT SETS ANY
  OTHER STATUS. Approval is a curator action performed outside the public app.
  Pre-screening is automatic but ADVISORY ONLY — it can flag a likely
  duplicate, a missing field, a location that does not look like Sikkim, or an
  obvious spam pattern. It cannot mark anything verified and it cannot reject
  anything; a cultural verification decision is a human decision. Storage is a
  JSON file + uploads dir under .data/, deliberately outside public/, so an
  unreviewed upload is never served as a static asset. Max 8 MB,
  jpeg/png/webp/avif. Supabase is the intended eventual home.

AGENT PIPELINE — scripts/ (23 scripts; npm run agent:*)
  agent:discover  heritage-discovery.mjs — walks Wikipedia categories and
                  Commons, cross-checks fields, scores confidence, stores
                  evidence. Records that cannot be corroborated are emitted as
                  candidates for human review, not published.
  agent:360       heritage-360-finder.mjs — Street View Static metadata
                  endpoint (the only sanctioned availability check). Records
                  an explicit NEGATIVE when none is found; a negative is a
                  valid answer here. Scraping Maps instead would breach terms.
  agent:audio     heritage-audio-agent.mjs — 60–90s guides composed PER
                  LANGUAGE in audio-scripts.mjs rather than translated across
                  them. Each guide ships with QA metadata: duration, word
                  count, signal peak/RMS/silence/clipping, a language-purity
                  check, the voice and engine used, and a `machineGenerated`
                  flag the player discloses.
  agent:gallery   heritage-gallery-agent.mjs — evidenced Commons photography.
  agent:archive   heritage-archive-agent.mjs — refuses to emit an item unless
                  three things resolve: the subject's reference article, a
                  Commons file, and a readable licence with an author.
  agent:research  sikkim-cultural-research.mjs → research/ + reports/
  agent:video, agent:reviews, agent:report (data-quality-report.mjs)
  images:vendor / images:optimize / images:credits — vendor Commons files into
                  public/ with credits, rather than hotlinking.
  export-curated.mjs keeps the agents reading the REVIEWED records the site
                  actually publishes, so slugs and facts stay in step.
  Output lands in reports/ and src/data/generated/, both committed as an audit
  trail.

THE GALLERY-REJECTION STORY — worth understanding, because it is the clearest
statement of this project's method and the QA harness's limits:

  The gallery agent accepts a Commons file on three kinds of evidence: the file
  sits in a category named for the subject, its title names the subject, or it
  carries GPS within metres of the subject. Two of those three fail in ways the
  agent cannot see.

    NAMED evidence matches on substring. "Soreng" matched Monchaux-Soreng, a
    commune in Seine-Maritime, France — and the gallery for a Sikkim town
    filled with a French wind turbine, a French factory and a French church.
    "Mangan" matched "Manganese", and a photograph of bacterial deposits on
    rocks in Kilbirnie, North Ayrshire was served on /hotels as the
    illustration for Mangan district. "Rongli" matched a school gate in
    Guangzhou.

    GEO evidence proves where the camera stood, not what it was pointed at. A
    butterfly photographed two metres from the Chungthang coordinate is
    evidence of a butterfly.

  gallery-integrity.mjs checked licence, authorship, file existence and
  duplicate use. It never checked that the photograph showed the place, so it
  passed all of this 11/11. The fix was a hand-reviewed deny-list
  (src/data/gallery-rejections.ts), and place/soreng now ends with an EMPTY
  gallery. An empty gallery is a true statement about what has been found;
  four photographs of Normandy are not.

  LESSON TO CARRY FORWARD: a green QA run proves the checks passed, not that
  the content is right. When adding a data source, ask what the checker
  structurally cannot see.

                  licensed collection — 35 sites were swept to establish that,

                  `translationReviewed` and `machineGenerated` flags and the
                  player discloses both. Four languages are blocked outright
                  with named blockers (§5, audio.ts).

STAY COMMERCIALS  Name and district only, marked unverified. Prices, ratings,
                  reviews, room inventory and precise coordinates all need a
                  licensed Places or booking integration.

TSD FUND TOTAL    Unpublished by the government; rendered "Data not available".

VISITING HOURS    Not captured for most sites; the availability panel says so.

SUPABASE          supabase/schema.sql defines only `monasteries` and `stays`,
                  both RLS-enabled with a public SELECT-only policy, provenance
                  columns travelling with each row. bookings, tsd_transactions
                  and tourism_stats were dropped with the synthetic data they
                  held. seed.sql upserts on slug. src/lib/supabase.ts returns
                  null when unconfigured and the app renders FULLY without a
                  database — Supabase is an optional overlay, never a hard
                  dependency. NOTE: the claude.ai Supabase MCP connector is
                  currently unauthorized in this workspace.

                  NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL, NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY

                  GOOGLE_MAPS_API_KEY (agents only, never the app),

                  reproducible re-runs). The app builds and deploys with the
