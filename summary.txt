NEY HERITAGE — PROJECT SUMMARY
Digitizing the Sacred Heritage of Sikkim
Last updated: 2026-08-16 · branch: main · working tree: uncommitted

================================================================================
1 · WHAT THIS IS NOW
================================================================================

NEY Heritage (formerly "SikkimOS") is a digital cultural-heritage platform for
Sikkim: a sourced archive of the state's monasteries, an interactive heritage
map, a rule-based trip planner, a directory of registered stays, and a public
preservation dashboard that reports the archive's own coverage and gaps.

The defining change since the last summary is a DATA-INTEGRITY PASS. The
earlier build shipped a large amount of invented content — hotel tariffs, star
ratings, guest reviews, room inventories, 220 synthetic bookings, a TSD
remittance ledger, invented festival dates, visitor timings, phone numbers and
email addresses, plus European panoramas reused as Sikkim prayer halls. All of
it has been deleted rather than relabelled.

The operating rule is now: every factual claim rendered in the product points
at an entry in src/data/sources.ts, or it does not ship. Where a fact is
missing, the UI says so explicitly ("Data not available", "Not yet captured")
instead of estimating. The gaps are published on purpose — /preservation exists
to show them.

Positioning shifted with it: from "SaaS platform for Sikkim tourism" to a
heritage archive that is honest about what it holds. The revenue-dashboard
framing of the old Phase 2 brief is gone.

4 · DATA LAYER
================================================================================

src/data/sources.ts      Central source registry. 6 sources (Sikkim Tourist
                         Trade Rules 2025 press coverage, UNI corroboration,
                         2025 arrivals reporting, Wikipedia, Wikimedia
                         Commons, OpenStreetMap), each with what may legitimately
                         be cited from it. Provenance type: sourceId, sourceUrl,
                         verifiedAt, confidence (high/medium/unverified),
                         optional caveat.

src/data/monasteries.ts  15 monasteries: rumtek, pemayangtse, tashiding,
                         enchey, lingdum, phodong, phensang, lachen, lachung,
                         sanga-choeling, dubdi, ralang, kewzing, rinchenpong,
                         tsuklakhang. Names, districts, lineages, founding
                         years and history from cited Wikipedia articles.
                         Coordinates only where the article publishes one;
                         sites with disputed coordinates are marked unverified
                         and are NOT plotted rather than given a guessed pin
                         (mappableMonasteries is the map's only input).
                         Every record reports tour: { available: false } and
                         audio: { available: false }.

src/data/places.ts       9 non-monastery heritage/natural sites, each with a
                         Wikipedia article and its published coordinate. No
                         "coming soon" padding. Permit notes where documented.

src/data/hotels.ts       20-entry stay directory: slug, name, district,
                         indicative tier, Google Maps search URL, provenance
                         marked "unverified" with an explicit caveat. Nothing
                         commercial.

src/data/images.ts       Keyed image registry (hero/, mon/, pano/, place/,
                         arch/, fest/, attr/) resolved through img().

src/lib/stats.ts         Verified tourism metrics: 2025 arrivals 17,12,360
                         total / 16,35,650 domestic / 61,710 foreign with 2024
                         comparisons. TSD Fund collected is value: null —
                         the fund exists but no collection total is published,
                         so it renders "Data not available" instead of an
                         estimate. (The old build reported 21,47,892 arrivals
                         and ₹12.5 Cr TSD; both were invented.)

5 · TSD — CORRECTED
================================================================================

src/lib/booking.ts now models the Tourism Sustainability Development Fund as
the Government of Sikkim actually defines it:

  • Flat ₹50 per person, one-time, valid one month
  • Collected by hotels at check-in, deposited to the TSD Fund
  • Children under 5 and government-work visitors exempt
  • Re-entry within the same month is charged again
  • Source: Sikkim Registration of Tourist Trade Rules, 2025

The previous implementation charged 2–4% of room tariff. That was invented and
misstated live public policy; it has been removed. GST is applied at 12% and
labelled an estimate, not a quoted rate. computeTariff(), nightsBetween() and
makeBookingId() ("NEY-HTL-####", deterministic) remain.

================================================================================
6 · TRIP PLANNER
================================================================================

src/lib/generate-itinerary.ts is a rule-based generator over real geography.
Four anchors — Gangtok, Pelling, Lachung, Ravangla — each with interest-tagged
day templates (Monasteries, Culture, Lakes, Food, Adventure, Trekking, Nature).
routePlan() chooses the anchor sequence and night allocation from trip length
and interests, keeping drive times sane; arrival days are always sequenced
first. Duration is clamped to 3–14 days.

Cost output deliberately contains ONE line: the statutory TSD fee.
Accommodation, transport and activity totals are not estimated, since no rates
feed is licensed. The return shape (GeneratedItinerary) is the contract a model
would fill in a later phase.

8 · TOOLING
================================================================================

scripts/heritage-discovery.mjs (290 lines) — a heritage discovery agent that
walks Wikipedia categories and Wikimedia Commons, cross-checks fields, scores
confidence, and stores evidence. It writes no prose of its own: every field is
copied from a named source with its URL and read date. Records that cannot be
corroborated are emitted as candidates for human review, not published.
Outputs: src/data/generated/monasteries.discovered.json,
reports/monastery-discovery.json.
