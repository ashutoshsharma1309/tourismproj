## This supersedes the statement this repository was built for

`pitch.txt` opens by naming **SIH260542 — "Digitize and Showcase Monasteries of
Sikkim for Tourism and Cultural Preservation."** That is not the current
statement and must not be quoted as one. The change is material:

| | SIH260542 (old) | 26202 (current) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Named subject | Monasteries of Sikkim | The tourism **industries** |
| Named actors | — | **hotels, travel, and others** |
| Stated verb | *digitize and showcase* | ***boost the current situation of*** |
| Scope | One state, one heritage class | Open; entrant chooses the ground |
| Beneficiary implied | Visitors and the cultural record | The tourism **trade** |

The old statement pointed at the demand side — help a visitor find and
understand heritage. The current statement points at the **supply side**: the
businesses that constitute the tourism industry. A monastery archive does not,
on its own, answer it.

## Derived requirements

The description is one sentence, so requirements are derived by reading its
terms rather than invented. Each row records what the wording licenses and what
it does not.

| # | Requirement, read from the wording | What it licenses | What it does not license |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | *"the tourism industries"* — plural, and *industries* not *tourists* | Work aimed at the businesses that supply tourism | Reading this as purely tourist-facing |
| R2 | *"including hotels"* — hotels named first and explicitly | Accommodation supply is in scope by name | Ignoring stays as "not heritage" |
| R3 | *"travel"* — named second | Transport, operators, agents, permits, movement | Restricting to a single mode |
| R4 | *"and others"* — deliberately open | Guides, food, crafts, homestays, transporters | Treating the list as closed at two |
| R5 | *"boost"* — a change verb, not a display verb | An intervention that improves something measurable | Shipping a brochure and calling it a boost |
| R6 | *"the current situation"* — the state of things **now** | Evidence about the present condition of the trade | Generic tourism-promotion content |
| R7 | Category **Software** | A working software artefact is the deliverable | Hardware, policy paper, or slide deck alone |
| R8 | Theme **Travel & Tourism** | Domain is fixed | Drifting into an adjacent theme |
| R9 | Title **Student Innovation**, dept **MIC-Student Innovation** | Novelty is graded explicitly; MIC judges idea originality | A competent clone of an existing product |
| R10 | No dataset link supplied | The entrant must source and defend their own data | Claiming an official dataset that was not given |

### The load-bearing phrase

**"boost the current situation."** It carries two obligations at once:

1. You must be able to state what *the current situation* actually is — with
   evidence, for a named part of the industry.
2. You must show the delta your software creates against that stated baseline.

A project that cannot articulate (1) cannot claim (2). This is the criterion
most tourism entries will fail, because "the current situation" is usually
asserted rather than measured.

### Target users implied by the wording

Primary (named or implied by *"industries"*): hoteliers and homestay
operators; travel agents and tour operators; transporters and drivers; guides;
the state tourism department that licenses all of the above.

Secondary (implied by any tourism product): the visitor, whose behaviour is the
mechanism through which the industry is boosted.

Note the ordering. Under SIH260542 the visitor was the subject. Under 26202 the
visitor is the *instrument*.

### Evaluation criteria

The portal publishes none for this statement. AICTE MIC Student Innovation
submissions are conventionally assessed on novelty, technical feasibility,
scale of impact, and implementability. Those are stated here as convention, not
as sourced criteria, and no scoring weights are claimed.

### Constraints

- Software (R7).
- No dataset provided (R10) — data sourcing is the entrant's burden and, given
  R6, part of what is being judged.
- No YouTube reference, no contact, no departmental brief. There is no further
  official material to consult; this one sentence is the whole statement.
